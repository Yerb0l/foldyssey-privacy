Foldyssey — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android application app.foldyssey.
Effective date: 1 November 2026. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Summary. Foldyssey has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not collect, receive or transmit any of your data. The screen image the app uses for its animation is processed in memory on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere.

1. Who is responsible

Foldyssey is developed and published by an individual developer. For any question about this policy or about data handling in the app, write to anissov.yerbol@gmail.com.

2. What Foldyssey is

Foldyssey is a visual effect for Samsung Galaxy Z Fold phones. When you fold or unfold the phone, it plays a short "frosted glass" transition over the screen: the current picture appears behind glass that blurs and darkens with the hinge angle, and clears when the phone is fully open or closed.

3. Accessibility service

Foldyssey uses the Android AccessibilityService API. It is not an assistive tool for people with disabilities; the API is used because it is the only way an app can draw an overlay above other apps during the fold/unfold transition and get an image of the current screen for that overlay. You turn the service on yourself in Android settings, after Foldyssey explains in the app what it does and asks for your consent. You can turn it off at any time in the same place, or pause the effect with the Quick Settings tile.

While the service is on, Foldyssey uses it only for the following:

Foldyssey does not read the text or contents of other apps' screens, does not perform actions on your behalf, does not change your settings through the accessibility service, and does not monitor what you type.

4. Optional "precise mode"

If you choose to enable precise mode, Foldyssey connects to your phone's own Wireless debugging service (Developer options), using a pairing code that you enter. This connection stays inside your phone and your local network; nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Over it, the app:

  • reads only the system log lines from the hinge sensor and the Samsung wallpaper service that contain the hinge angle;
  • grants itself the permission to switch Wireless debugging back on after a restart, if you allow automatic reconnection.

A private key used to authenticate this connection is generated on your phone and stored in the app's private storage. The pairing code is not stored or logged.

6. Data the developer collects

None. Foldyssey contains no analytics framework, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, no tracking SDK and no telemetry of any kind. The developer receives no information about you, your device or your usage, and therefore has nothing to sell, share or disclose. If you choose to send the diagnostics log to the developer, it is used only to fix and tune the app.

Foldyssey does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, calendar, location, phone state, call logs, SMS, files, health data or the list of installed apps.

8. Children

Foldyssey is a visual customisation for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

9. Retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for the developer to retain or delete. On your phone, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Foldyssey, removes the settings, the diagnostics log and the precise mode key.
