Foldyssey — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android application app.foldyssey.
Effective date: 1 November 2026. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Summary. Foldyssey has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not collect, receive or transmit any of your data. The screen image the app uses for its animation is processed in memory on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere.

1. Who is responsible

Foldyssey is developed and published by an individual developer. For any question about this policy or about data handling in the app, write to anissov.yerbol@gmail.com.

2. What Foldyssey is

Foldyssey is a visual effect for Samsung Galaxy Z Fold phones. When you fold or unfold the phone, it plays a short "frosted glass" transition over the screen: the current picture appears behind glass that blurs and darkens with the hinge angle, and clears when the phone is fully open or closed.

3. Accessibility service

Foldyssey uses the Android AccessibilityService API. It is not an assistive tool for people with disabilities; the API is used because it is the only way an app can draw an overlay above other apps during the fold/unfold transition and get an image of the current screen for that overlay. Before you can open the accessibility settings from the app, Foldyssey shows a separate screen that explains what the service accesses and why, and asks for your consent with "Agree" or "Not now". Until you tap "Agree", the service does nothing, even if you turn it on directly in Android settings: it asks Android not to send it any window events, takes no screenshots, reads no windows or app names and shows no overlay. You can read that explanation again and withdraw your consent at any time from the menu on the app's main screen; the service stops at once. You can also turn the service off in Android settings, or pause the effect with the Quick Settings tile.

4. Optional "precise mode"

Precise mode is off unless you choose it. Before it starts, Foldyssey shows a separate screen that explains what precise mode does and asks for your consent. If you agree, Foldyssey connects only to your phone's own Wireless debugging service (Developer options), using a pairing code that you type yourself, and checks that the other end really is your phone's system (for this check it briefly writes its own key foldyssey_adb_probe to the system settings and deletes it right away). The connection itself stays inside your phone; to find it, the app sends standard service-discovery (mDNS) queries on your Wi-Fi network, which carry no personal data. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Over the connection, the app:

  • reads only the system log lines from the hinge sensor and the Samsung fold wallpaper that contain the hinge angle (the log is filtered by these two sources);
  • only if you turn on "Turn Wireless debugging on automatically" (off by default), grants itself the WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS permission and uses it only to switch Wireless debugging back on after a restart or when Wi-Fi returns. It changes no other settings with this permission.

While precise mode reads the angle from the Samsung fold wallpaper and the screen is on, Foldyssey keeps an invisible 1×1-pixel window (it does not take touches) and asks the wallpaper for the angle about 60 times a second. The window is removed when the screen turns off, when precise mode or the effect is turned off, or if the wallpaper does not answer.

While Wireless debugging is on, a computer that you have already paired with your phone can also connect to it on the same network; you can turn Wireless debugging off in Developer options at any time. You can withdraw your consent for precise mode from the menu on the app's main screen: Foldyssey then disconnects and returns to basic mode.

A private key used to authenticate this connection is generated on your phone and stored in the app's private storage. The pairing code is not stored or logged.

6. Data the developer collects

None. Foldyssey contains no analytics framework, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, no tracking SDK and no telemetry of any kind. The developer receives no information about you, your device or your usage, and therefore has nothing to sell, share or disclose. If you choose to send the diagnostics log to the developer, it is used only to fix and tune the app.

Foldyssey does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, calendar, location, phone state, call logs, SMS, files, health data or the list of installed apps. It does not ask to be exempted from battery optimization: the setup screen only opens Android's battery optimization list, where you decide yourself.

8. Children

Foldyssey is a visual customisation for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

9. Retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for the developer to retain or delete. On your phone, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Foldyssey, removes the settings, the diagnostics log and the precise mode key.
